THE RESEARCH CONTRACT

You bring the policy.
The lab supplies the traffic world.

Every method is evaluated inside the same vehicle dynamics, resource-access rules, task streams, maps, and metric definitions. That turns an algorithmic idea into comparable system-level evidence—without rebuilding the simulator for every study.

OBSERVE

Shared traffic state

Tracks, vehicles, tasks, queues, regions, occupancy, and demand.

→
DECIDE

Your method

Return complete routes or regulate admission and empty-vehicle supply.

→
MEASURE

System outcomes

Throughput, cycle time, congestion, utilization, and loss of liveness.

ONE BENCHMARK, TWO CONTROL SURFACES

Routing chooses a path.
Regional control keeps it operable.

Vehicle-level decisions and network-level regulation are evaluated inside the same traffic system.

TEST BEYOND ONE OPERATING POINT

Three maps.
Seven scenarios.

The Small map is a compact production corridor, Medium introduces repeated Bays around a shared spine, and Large extends the network and regional interactions. The same routing method and regional-control policy can be applied to every map. Each map, however, has its own embedded task-generation defaults; those benchmark demand settings are fixed.
