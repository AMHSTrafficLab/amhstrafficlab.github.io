REGIONAL-CONTROL EFFECT

Regional-control results

Across the tested routing methods and operating scenarios, regional admission substantially reduces runs with throughput collapse or confirmed circular waits. Without regional control, most confirmed circular waits remain inside a single Bay; some extend across a larger part of the network.

The detector reconstructs waiting dependencies and reports the number and spatial extent of vehicles involved in a confirmed cycle.

EMPTY-VEHICLE REBALANCING

Results by operating point

Under Dijkstra routing, mean cycle time decreases in the two Large scenarios and increases in SMALL 150.

Throughput remains stable, utilization falls, and the travel-time index can rise slightly. The result suggests that the main effect is vehicle availability rather than shorter travel paths in every scenario.
